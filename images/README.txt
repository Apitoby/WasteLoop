WasteLoop demo photos
=====================

Put your own photos here to replace the drawn symbols on the demo
marketplace items and the SDP Showcase projects. Every phone that opens
the app will see them.

- Name the file exactly as listed below.
- .jpg, .jpeg, .png or .webp all work.
- Keep each photo under about 500 KB. Square or 4:3 photos look best.
- If a file is missing, the app shows the drawn symbol instead.

How to upload on GitHub: open the images/market (or images/showcase)
folder in your repo, click "Add file" > "Upload files", drag the photos
in, then "Commit changes". Vercel updates the app in about a minute.

Quick alternative (this phone only): sign in as SDP staff and use
"Add" under Photo on the Listings page, or "Before / after photos" on
the Showcase page.
